# Exhaust the Design Space

**Apply when:** a design is expensive to reverse, lacks a strong repository precedent, and admits several structurally different shapes.

Produce at least two genuinely different designs or prototypes before selecting one. Whole-shape alternatives vary ownership, interface, data model, or interaction; changing a method name inside one architecture is not an alternative.

For module interfaces, call the Skill tool with `codebase-design` and use its Design It Twice process. For observable state or UI questions, call the Skill tool with `prototype`. Compare alternatives by the decisions they expose, not by presentation polish.

Skip this for mechanical work and decisions already settled by a strong local precedent. Exploration earns its cost only before a consequential choice closes options.

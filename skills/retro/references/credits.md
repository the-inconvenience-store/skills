# Sources and adaptations

The diagnostic reference paraphrases techniques from these primary skill documents, consulted on 2026-11-10. Links follow upstream branches and may change; upstream empirical examples are not measurements of the audited repository.

- **[2btwist/dotagents: agent-latency-audit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/2btwist/dotagents/HEAD/skills/agent-latency-audit/SKILL.md).** Adapted duration bands, distribution inspection, frequency-first leverage, approval investigation, shell-startup comparisons, polling, context pressure, and config/habit/runtime classification. Here bimodality and turnaround gaps generate hypotheses, not phase attribution. Approval needs explicit boundaries; shell initialization needs a safety review. Savings remain call-time estimates unless dependency evidence supports wall-clock impact. Permission changes are proposals, not audit actions.
- **[greglas75/zuvo: profile-session](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/greglas75/zuvo/HEAD/skills/profile-session/SKILL.md).** Adapted reproducible classifiers and token/cache calculations, largest-gap inspection, duplicate-analysis avoidance, comparable before/after views, and explicit exoneration of cheap quality gates. Reuse is keyed by source contents, window, and method rather than cwd or path alone. We do not charge a gap to the preceding activity, assume away-time thresholds, require its profiler, or adopt its installation, retrospective, logging, and report-path mandates.
- **[luoyuctl/agenttrace: agenttrace-session-audit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/luoyuctl/agenttrace/master/skills/agenttrace-session-audit/SKILL.md).** Adapted capability-aware interpretation, tool-failure/retry investigation, privacy, and explicit missing metrics. Aggregate or metadata-only records cannot establish event-level timing. AgentTrace is an optional evidence source, not a prerequisite; health scores and CI thresholds do not replace this audit's time ledger or authorize changes.
- **[hoangsonww/Claude-Code-Agent-Monitor: session-debug](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hoangsonww/Claude-Code-Agent-Monitor/master/plugins/ccam-devtools/skills/session-debug/SKILL.md).** Adapted lifecycle/event-chain inspection, recursive agent trees, failure context, retry patterns, compaction context, and dependency investigation. Agent Monitor is used only when existing local telemetry is available, through discovered configuration and read-only access. No installation, assumed endpoint, automatic idle attribution, or critical-path claim from timestamps alone is adopted.

These are diagnostic references, not adopted harness requirements. Mode selection and proposal boundaries live in [retro](../SKILL.md); full session discovery and timing rules live in [time-audit.md](time-audit.md). Shared diagnosis is available to both modes.

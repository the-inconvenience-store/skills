# Guard the Context Window

**Apply when:** a phase boundary, long investigation, large output, or parallel fan-out threatens the context needed for sound judgment.

Keep primary-source reasoning in the current session while the next phase still depends on it and the work fits. Delegate independent bulk reading or checks, then retain compact findings and artifact pointers rather than raw output.

Move context only at a phase boundary. Use a handoff when the work must travel to another harness, directory, colleague, or forked side task; use a fresh ticket context when the ticket is self-contained.

Summaries are lossy. Do not compact or hand off merely to make the window look tidy, and do not keep bulk payloads in the lead session when only their conclusions are needed.
